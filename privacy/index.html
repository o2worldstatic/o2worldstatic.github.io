Privacy Policy

Effective August 1, 2026

1. Categories of information collected

Account data — the email address by which you sign in to, and verify, your account.

Purchase records — the fact that a Nova Pass was purchased, and the account to which it was attributed. No card details or payment credentials are collected, viewed, or stored by us at any time; these are submitted directly to Stripe on its own hosted checkout page, which is not embedded in, and shares no code with, any page of O2World.

Gameplay analytics — events such as the commencement of a level or a multiplayer run, collected by means of Google Analytics (GA4) for the purpose of understanding usage of O2World.

2. Disclosure to third parties

Stripe, for purposes of payment processing — the email address associated with your account is transmitted with the payment so that the purchase can be attributed to it, and your purchase is additionally subject to Stripe's own privacy policy, available at stripe.com/privacy. Google Analytics, for the gameplay analytics described above. Your data is not sold, and is not disclosed to any party beyond the foregoing processors, except where disclosure is required by applicable law.

3. Cookies and local storage

Google Analytics sets cookies and/or local storage entries for purposes of usage measurement. A comprehensive cookie-consent mechanism has not yet been implemented; this fact is disclosed here rather than represented otherwise.

4. Data subject rights

Residents of the European Union or United Kingdom are entitled to access, export, or delete their data pursuant to the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Residents of California are afforded comparable rights pursuant to the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). Any such right may be exercised by contacting contact@o2jam.world.

5. Retention and international transfer

Account and purchase data is retained for so long as the account in question remains in existence. Such data may be processed on servers located outside the data subject's country of residence.

6. Children's privacy

O2World is not directed to, or intended for use by, children under the age of 13.

7. Amendment of this policy

This policy may be amended from time to time in our discretion. Any such amendment shall take effect immediately upon being posted to this page.

8. Notices and inquiries

Privacy-related inquiries or requests should be directed to: contact@o2jam.world.
