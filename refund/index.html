Refund Policy

Effective August 1, 2026

1. Payment processing

Payments for the Nova Pass are processed by Stripe, acting solely as payment processor. O2World is the seller of record with respect to such transactions, and shall accordingly be identified as the merchant on the purchaser's receipt or account statement and shall be responsible for the calculation and remittance of any tax applicable to the transaction. The responsibility of Stripe is limited to the mechanics of processing the payment, and it is not a party to the sale. Payment card details are collected and retained by Stripe on its own hosted checkout page and are at no point received, transmitted, or stored by O2World.

2. Sales deemed final

The Nova Pass constitutes digital content that is delivered, and access to which is unlocked, immediately upon completion of purchase. In consideration of such immediate delivery, all Nova Pass purchases are sold on a final basis, and no refund shall be issued on account of a change of mind or similar discretionary request.

3. Exceptions and disputes

A purchaser who was charged without receiving the corresponding access, charged more than once in respect of the same pass, or who has reason to believe a charge to be fraudulent, is invited to contact contact@o2jam.world so that the matter may be resolved. As O2World is the seller of record, such requests are determined by O2World rather than by the payment processor; a purchaser retains any right to raise a dispute with the issuer of the payment method used. Where a refund is issued or a charge is reversed for any reason whatsoever, the Nova Pass access associated with the purchase in question shall be revoked concurrently.

4. EU/UK right of withdrawal

Purchasers located in the European Union or United Kingdom are ordinarily afforded, under applicable consumer protection law, a period of fourteen (14) days within which to withdraw from an online purchase. As the Nova Pass constitutes digital content delivered immediately upon purchase, the purchaser is required to expressly acknowledge such immediate delivery and to waive such right of withdrawal before the transaction may proceed. That acknowledgment is presented on the Nova Pass purchase page, and must be given before checkout may be commenced; it is this acknowledgment that renders the final-sale terms of Section 2 applicable notwithstanding EU/UK law.

5. Notices and inquiries

Billing inquiries should be directed to: contact@o2jam.world.
